AutoCura AI

OBD2 Diagnostics, Vehicle Health & Smart AI Automotive Assistant

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

1. About AutoCura AI

AutoCura AI is a modern automotive diagnostic and vehicle management application. It empowers vehicle owners to diagnose engine trouble codes (DTCs) via Bluetooth OBD2 adapters, view real-time engine telemetry (RPM, Speed, Coolant Temperature, Engine Load, Fuel Trim), log fuel and maintenance expenses, track vehicle inspection and insurance expiration dates, and get artificial intelligence-powered diagnostic explanations powered by Google Gemini.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information strictly required to deliver the features of AutoCura AI:

  • Account & Authentication Information: When you sign in with Google or Email/Password via Firebase Authentication, we collect your name, email address, and profile photo URL to manage your user profile and sync your garage vehicles across your devices. Guest mode users do not transmit personal identity data.
  • Vehicle & Garage Data: Information you enter about your vehicle (make, model, year, engine, license plate, mileage, inspection date, insurance policy dates).
  • OBD2 Diagnostic Logs: Diagnostic Trouble Codes (DTCs), sensor readings, and vehicle identification numbers (VIN) read during active OBD2 diagnostic scans.
  • Expense & Fuel Records: Fuel consumption logs, repair costs, and maintenance records you choose to track in the expense manager.
  • Media for AI Diagnosis: Photos of dashboard warning indicators or engine audio/video clips that you voluntarily submit for AI diagnostic analysis.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your identity and provide secure cloud backup of your garage vehicles and expense history via Google Firebase.
  • To display real-time live gauges and interpret diagnostic trouble codes (DTC) read from your vehicle's engine control unit (ECU).
  • To provide automated reminder notifications for upcoming mandatory vehicle inspections and insurance renewal dates.
  • To analyze vehicle symptoms and trouble codes using Google Vertex AI (Gemini) and generate actionable troubleshooting advice.

4. Data Storage, Security & Retention

Security is our utmost priority:

  • All network communications between the AutoCura AI application, Google Firebase, and Google Cloud endpoints are encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/SSL).
  • Cloud data is protected by strict Firebase Security Rules that ensure only you can access or modify your personal vehicle records.
  • You can use AutoCura AI in Guest Mode without creating an account; in this mode, all vehicle records and logs are stored exclusively in local device storage.

5. Data Deletion & User Rights

You have full control over your personal data at all times:

  • Deleting Vehicles & Expenses: You can delete any vehicle, expense, or diagnostic record directly from within the app at any time.
  • Account & Cloud Data Deletion: You can request immediate and permanent deletion of your account and all associated Firestore cloud records by contacting our support team at mustafabakan.app@gmail.com. Upon receipt, all user data will be permanently wiped within 48 hours.
  • Local Data Removal: Uninstalling the application immediately removes all locally cached data and preferences from your device.

6. Third-Party Services & Permissions

AutoCura AI integrates with trusted Google services to ensure reliability and safety:

  • Google Firebase Authentication & Firestore: For secure user sign-in and encrypted cloud synchronization.
  • Google Gemini API / Vertex AI: For real-time automotive symptom analysis and troubleshooting recommendations.
  • Google AdMob: To display banner and interstitial advertisements supporting free access to core AI diagnostic features.
  • Device Permissions:
    • Bluetooth (Nearby Devices): Required to discover and connect to your Bluetooth OBD2 adapter.
    • Camera & Storage: Required only when you take or attach a photo of warning lights or documents for AI analysis.
    • Notifications: Required to deliver periodic vehicle inspection and insurance expiration reminders.

7. Terms of Service & Disclaimers

AutoCura AI provides diagnostic interpretations and AI-generated maintenance recommendations for informational and preventive assistance. AutoCura AI does not replace certified professional automotive service technicians. Always observe vehicle safety regulations and consult a qualified mechanic for severe mechanical or electrical malfunctions.
