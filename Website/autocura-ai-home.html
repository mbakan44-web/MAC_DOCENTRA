About AutoCura AI

AutoCura AI is an all-in-one smart automotive assistant and OBD2 diagnostic application designed for car owners, DIY mechanics, and automotive enthusiasts. Powered by Google Vertex AI (Gemini), AutoCura AI provides instant diagnostic insights, real-time live sensor gauges, fuel and maintenance logging, and automated inspection reminders.

Key Features

🔌 Live OBD2 Diagnostics

Read real-time engine RPM, speed, coolant temp, intake pressure (MAP), and mass airflow (MAF) via Bluetooth OBD2.

🧠 Gemini AI Symptom Analysis

Scan Check Engine fault codes (DTCs) and get clear, understandable diagnostic explanations powered by Google Gemini.

⛽ Fuel & Expense Tracking

Log fuel fill-ups, maintenance costs, and calculate detailed running expenses with intelligent fuel economy analysis.

🔔 Inspection & Insurance Alerts

Automated periodic reminders for mandatory vehicle inspections and traffic insurance policies.

☁️ Secure Cloud Backup

Encrypted garage sync with Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore across all your devices.

🧪 Built-in Demo Simulator

Test the full OBD2 diagnostic dashboard and AI scanner instantly without needing a physical vehicle or adapter.

Privacy & Data Protection

Your vehicle data and personal privacy are fully protected by end-to-end SSL encryption and strict Firebase security rules.
